Privacy Policy for Lakeville Roleplay Bot
Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Developed by: ALTIS Automations

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how ALTIS Automations collects, stores, uses, and protects data when you interact with the Lakeville Roleplay Bot ("the Bot") in the Lakeville Roleplay Discord server.

We are committed to maintaining user trust by keeping data collection strictly limited to what is necessary for server roleplay, administration, and support functions.

2. Information We Collect
When you interact with the Bot, the following data points may be collected and stored in our secure database:

Discord User Information: Your unique Discord User ID, username, and server display name/nickname.

Roblox Identity: Your linked Roblox username and public avatar headshot (retrieved via Roblox's public API) to populate profile cards and ticket panels.

Support Ticket Data: Ticket channel metadata, ticket categories, reason for opening, claim logs, staff notes, and full conversation transcripts generated upon ticket closure.

Roleplay Vehicle Records: Registered vehicle/trailer details, including model year, manufacturer, model name, exterior color, and license plate number tied to your Discord User ID.

Citation & Warrant Records: Citation IDs, cited civilian IDs, issuing officer IDs, penal codes, fine amounts, creation timestamps, due dates, reminder flags, and active warrant flags.

Server Suggestions: Suggestion text content, submitter Discord User ID, and corresponding suggestion message links.

3. How We Use Your Information
Data collected by the Bot is used solely to provide core community systems, including:

Managing and logging private staff support inquiries and reports.

Managing in-game civilian vehicle registries and enabling Law Enforcement plate searches (/plate).

Tracking roleplay citations, sending automated reminder Direct Messages, and alerting Law Enforcement to overdue warrants.

Displaying public user profiles and statistics within the server.

Maintaining server audit and security logs in designated staff channels.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute user data to any external commercial third parties.

Data is only transmitted to:

Discord API: To process application commands, send direct messages, and manage channel permissions.

Roblox API: To query public user headshot thumbnails and confirm usernames.

5. Data Retention and Security
Storage: All persistent data (records, citations, tickets, and whitelisted vehicles) is stored locally in an encrypted/protected SQLite database hosted on the bot’s private server.

Transcripts: Closed ticket transcripts are stored securely within dedicated, permission-restricted staff archive channels.

Data Lifespan: Active vehicle records remain until deleted via the /unregister command or manual removal by an administrator. Citation records remain until cleared by Law Enforcement High Rank staff.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests
You have the right to inspect or request the deletion of your stored records at any time:

Self-Service: You can remove your registered vehicles at any time using the /unregister command.

Manual Erasure Requests: To request a complete purge of your database entries (citations, active records, or ticket logs), you may submit an "Ask Staff" support ticket within the server or contact ALTIS Automations management.

7. Children's Privacy
The Bot complies with Discord's Minimum Age Requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13 years of age (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to This Policy
ALTIS Automations reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy as new bot systems or commands are released. Any updates will be noted with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
